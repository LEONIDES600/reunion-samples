02 · Bio

Cazey (ˈkeɪsi)

Music prod. & DJ

Cazey is an Italian music producer and DJ based in Milan, working across music production, beatmaking, sampling, sound design, audio engineering, and mixing. Her approach is deeply collaborative, working closely with artists to shape their sound and turn ideas into distinctive, forward-thinking records. As a DJ and Artist DJ, she brings the same musical identity and instinct to the stage curating dynamic sets, performing independently, and supporting artists throughout their live shows as artist DJ.
